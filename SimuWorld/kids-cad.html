What happens next?

Your chosen 3D draft opens in the regular SimuWorld CAD viewport. You can resize, rotate, recolor and continue engineering the overall object. Separate-part editing is a future upgrade. 3D printing is optional.

Optional 3D print export

3D printing is optional. This exports your selected model or the whole design as an STL mesh for a slicer. It does not start a printer.
